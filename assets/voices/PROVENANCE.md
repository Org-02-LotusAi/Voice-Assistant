| `james_calm.wav` / `.txt` | Windows SAPI "Microsoft David Desktop" | Synthetic OS TTS voice generated by operator with `scripts/make_seed_voices.ps1` | Authorized by construction: no real person's voice was recorded or cloned |

Format: mono, 16-bit PCM, 24 kHz, about 12 s each. The `.txt` file is the exact spoken transcript (Voicebox `reference_text`).

`assets/test_audio/` holds the human-side test utterances for the acceptance harness, rendered with SAPI "Microsoft Mark" (a third voice, distinct from both identity voices; 16 kHz mono 16-bit). They are never produced by the system under test.

These files are used to create the Voicebox cloned profiles "Ava Warm" and "James Calm" (`scripts/seed_voicebox_profiles.py`).
